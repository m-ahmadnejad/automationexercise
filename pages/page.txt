What is “Page” (Page Object) really?

It’s just a place to group:

locators
actions
-----
Why we use Page (real reason, not theory)
1) To remove repetition
2) To make tests readable like user behavior
3) To isolate UI changes
If tomorrow the selector changes:

<input name="email">

becomes:

<input id="user-email">

Without Page:
❌ you fix it in 10 tests

With Page:
✅ you fix it in one place
------------------------
Important: what Page is NOT
❌ not for assertions (usually)
❌ not for business logic
❌ not for everything in the project
❌ not required for small tests

It’s just:
👉 UI interaction layer
